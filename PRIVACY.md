# MikiConnect Privacy Notice — Draft

**Owner:** Mika Daniel (MIKI)
**Contact:** danmike849@gmail.com
**Effective date:** To be completed before public launch

This draft describes the categories of information MikiConnect may process. It must be reviewed and adapted to the actual business, hosting providers, legal requirements, retention practices, and jurisdiction before publication.

## Information processed
- Account information such as username and email address.
- Authentication information, including password hashes and token-version/session-security data.
- User-created content such as posts, comments, profile information, and messages.
- Safety and moderation information such as reports and administrative actions.
- Technical information required to operate the service, such as IP address and request metadata, subject to the final logging configuration.

## Purposes
MikiConnect may process this information to provide accounts and messaging, authenticate users, recover accounts, moderate abuse, secure the service, respond to support requests, and maintain the platform.

## Email communications
Transactional email may be sent for account verification and password recovery. The initial implementation is designed for Resend as the transactional email provider.

## Retention and deletion
Retention periods should be documented before launch. Users should be given a clear process for account/data deletion where required by applicable law.

## Security
MikiConnect uses password hashing, expiring single-use email tokens, JWT session controls, rate limiting, server-side authorization, and transport/security headers. No security system guarantees absolute security.

## Contact
Privacy requests may be sent to danmike849@gmail.com.
